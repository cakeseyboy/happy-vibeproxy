# Unofficial Happy + VibeProxy fork

This is an unofficial fork of [Happy](https://github.com/slopus/happy),
distributed under the original MIT license. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Happy Coder or Slopus.

The fork adds dynamic model discovery for VibeProxy/`claude-code-proxy` and
publishes tested builds from this repository. The original copyright and MIT
license are preserved in `LICENSE` and in the CLI package.
